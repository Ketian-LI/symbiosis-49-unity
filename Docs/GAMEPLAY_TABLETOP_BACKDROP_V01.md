# Gameplay tabletop backdrop v01

The gameplay camera renders a live, selectable 7×7 board over `Assets/Resources/UI/GameplayHud/gameplay-tabletop-backdrop-v01.png`. The background is a separate unlit world-space plane below the board, with no collider. This keeps room movement and mouse selection independent of the decorative tabletop.

The asset was generated by editing the user-supplied reference `exec-9ad5ed58-b224-430f-9fb5-6e2c0f40a12c.png`. The central fixed board was removed and replaced with blank paper so a duplicate, non-interactive board cannot show behind the real board. The surrounding wooden desk, plants, books, feather, coffee, leaves, and carved bird retain the reference's composition. The plane is rotated 180° about its vertical axis to compensate for Unity's top-down Plane UV orientation; this was checked with an actual Unity gameplay capture.
