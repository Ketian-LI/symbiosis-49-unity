# User testing method and record

## Participants and setting

Participants were acquaintances invited privately. They played remotely on their own computers.
The no-camera Fill game was the main condition.

| Round | Participants | Recorded account |
| --- | ---: | --- |
| First | 5 | A 49-day task, written and voice feedback, and 3 returned questionnaires. |
| Second | 13 | Further feedback using revised waste and turn rules. |
| Across both | 13 different people | The original 5 returned; 8 more joined the second round. |

Participant counts do not establish completion of every checkpoint or a full 49-day run.
The public record does not identify the exact executable used in every session.

The second-round rules included waste collection across the eight neighbouring plots, a penalty for a home
sharing an edge with a waste facility, and turn scoring triggered by card placement.

## Questions and recording

The first round asked participants to complete the game and suggest improvements.
The second-round form asked about four moments: finishing the fixed-card opening, making the first free choice,
changing a plan later in the game and reaching the end. It also asked participants to judge two layouts.

The form separated the choice, expected result, noticed result and participant explanation.
Its checkpoints describe the recording design; they do not establish that every response was collected at that moment.

## Interpretation and limits

Recruitment through personal contacts limits the range of views represented. The two rounds also used different rules
and recording methods. They cannot isolate the effect of a single change. Later stories, highlights and card layouts
are not validated by feedback from an older build.

The public archive contains the method and evaluation account, rather than a complete participant-level dataset.
It therefore does not support detailed comparisons of assistance, missing answers or completion across individuals.

The study began before formal ethics review. Permitted further use of the earlier records remains unresolved,
as documented in the [ethics reflection](../../ETHICS_REFLECTION.md). Private forms, messages and audio are excluded
from this repository.
